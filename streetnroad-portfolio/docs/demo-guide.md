# Demonstration Guide

## Recommended public walkthrough

A short demonstration can show the product experience without exposing the production environment.

1. Open the StreetNRoad landing or discovery page.
2. Browse a sample business category.
3. Open a sample business profile.
4. Show a sample gallery and contact pathway.
5. Demonstrate a sample invoice or receipt workflow using fictional data.
6. Explain the WhatsApp notification flow at a high level.
7. Show a conceptual receipt verification experience using a synthetic reference.
8. Close with the product vision and the technology areas involved.

## Recording checklist

Before recording or taking screenshots:

- Use a demo account, not a production administrator account.
- Use fictional business and customer details.
- Remove browser bookmarks and tabs that reveal private systems.
- Hide address bars if they contain internal URLs or query tokens.
- Check screenshots for email addresses, phone numbers, IDs and QR codes that resolve to real records.
- Do not show passwords, access tokens, cookies, API responses containing secrets or server terminals.
- Do not demonstrate access to a real bank or customer system.
