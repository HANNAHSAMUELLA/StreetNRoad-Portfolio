# Product Capabilities

This catalogue describes StreetNRoad's product areas at a portfolio level.

## Business discovery and profiles
- Browse businesses and service categories.
- View business information and profile content.
- Present photos and galleries.
- Provide direct contact pathways, including WhatsApp where configured.
- Support location-aware discovery concepts.

## Business promotion
- Business-facing tools for managing presence and promotional content.
- Advertising-oriented presentation of participating businesses.
- Promotional placements and discovery experiences.

## Events and property listings
- Listing concepts for local events.
- Real-estate discovery and listing workflows.

## Invoice, receipt and memo SaaS
- Business document workflows for invoices and receipts.
- Receipt and invoice document generation.
- WhatsApp notification integration using approved templates and configured business accounts.
- QR-based receipt verification concepts.
- Tenant-oriented workflows for organizations using the service.

**Important:** This is a document and messaging workflow, not a payment gateway or a connection into a customer's core banking system.

## Delivery and tracking
- Order and vendor workflow concepts.
- Driver location updates.
- Customer-facing delivery status and tracking.

## Administration
- Operational dashboards and administrative workflows.
- Business and content management concepts.
- Role-based workflow direction for organizational users.

## Demonstration guidance

Use fictional businesses, synthetic contact details and sample documents in all public demonstrations. Do not publish actual customer receipts, transaction references, bank records, WhatsApp identifiers or personal information.
