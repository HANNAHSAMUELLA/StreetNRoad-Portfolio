# StreetNRoad — Platform Overview

## Vision

StreetNRoad aims to make it easier to discover businesses and connect customers with the services available around them. It is designed with local business visibility and practical digital workflows in mind, beginning with Sierra Leone.

## The problem space

Small and growing businesses can have difficulty maintaining a useful online presence, being discovered by new customers and managing routine digital interactions. Customers, in turn, may have to search across disconnected channels to find reliable business information.

StreetNRoad's product direction is to bring business profiles, discovery, contact pathways and selected business tools into a coherent digital experience.

## Product principles

- **Direct discovery:** Make business information easier to find.
- **Business ownership:** Give businesses a place to present their own information.
- **Practical communication:** Support direct contact pathways such as WhatsApp.
- **Local relevance:** Design around local businesses, services and users.
- **Modular growth:** Develop separate capabilities that can evolve without exposing unnecessary complexity to users.

## Product ecosystem

### 1. Business discovery
A discovery experience for browsing participating businesses and service categories.

### 2. Business presence
Business pages can present descriptions, contact information, images and galleries.

### 3. Promotion and advertising
Tools intended to help businesses promote their presence and reach potential customers.

### 4. Events and property
Listing concepts for events and real-estate opportunities.

### 5. Invoice, receipt and memo workflows
A separate business SaaS direction focused on creating and managing documents and sending supported notifications through WhatsApp Business Platform integrations. It is designed as a workflow tool, not as a payment processor.

### 6. Delivery and tracking
A delivery workflow direction involving order coordination, driver location updates and customer-facing tracking.

## Intended audiences

- People discovering local businesses and services
- Small and medium-sized businesses
- Organizations that need structured invoice and receipt workflows
- Businesses coordinating deliveries

## Scope note

This document describes the product direction and areas of development. It is not a service-level commitment, security certification, financial-services claim or statement that every module is generally available.
